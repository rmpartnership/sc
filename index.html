How to use this scorecardQuick tips for reading the cards, labels and charts
  • Start with the three numbers above for the big picture.
  • Select a person in the milestone graphic, or a row in the Snapshot table, to open that milestone’s page. Each page starts with where we are now (the key number, its trend and the 2030 target), then explains what the data tells us, how we measure it, why it matters and what’s impacting it. Use Next, or the row of people at the top of each milestone page, to move between milestones.
  • Each card’s label shows at a glance whether a result is improving, not improving, holding steady, based on older data, or has no data yet. Changes in percentages are shown in percentage points (pts).
  • Use Search & filters at the top to compare specific metrics or show only ‘Big 3’ metrics.
  • Select Explore the data on a card for its full chart, exact numbers, notes and source, or open the Full Progress Table tab to see every metric in one table.
  • Hover over (or tap) a chart, bar or asterisk (*) to see exact values and notes.

Tracking Our Progress Towards 71,142

Rocky Mountain Partnership Cradle to Career™ has set an ambitious target to ensure an additional 71,142 children and young people (up to age 35) who are not currently meeting Cradle to Career™ milestones, achieve these critical steps for upward mobility by 2030. This target is not just a number—it represents our commitment to transforming lives and strengthening our community.

This work is RMP's contribution to StriveTogether's commitment to 4 million young people across the U.S. achieving economic mobility by 2030. The table below tracks our progress metric by metric: how many additional young people are meeting each milestone now, where we projected we would be at this point, and what we need to reach by 2030-2031.

Regions across Colorado are working together toward one shared goal: 162,000 more young people meeting Cradle to Career milestones by 2030. Rocky Mountain Partnership Cradle to Career™ leads this work in the north metro region (Adams County, Arvada, Aurora, Broomfield) as part of Cradle to Career™ Colorado.

That statewide goal is part of StriveTogether’s goal of 4 million more young people on a path to upward mobility by 2030.

RMP Cradle to Career Outcome Metrics · Progress Towards our 71K Goal. Charts show actual data (teal), what happens if nothing changes (orange), and the path to our 2030-31 target (gray). ‘Big 3’-aligned metrics are marked with the BIG 3 icon.

Please note: Some forecasts are limited due to limited historical data. CMAS assessments (third grade reading and eighth grade math) were not conducted in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and data sourced from the US Census Bureau is not collected during census years (like 2020).
